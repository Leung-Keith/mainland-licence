FAQ

Do I need to take a test with a Hong Kong licence?

No. Hong Kong licence holders with a Mainland Travel Permit only need a medical check. We bring you to an automated booth and it takes about 10 minutes.

Can non-Chinese Hong Kong residents apply?

Yes, if you hold the Mainland Travel Permit for non-Chinese Hong Kong residents.

Can I apply with a foreign licence?

Yes, if you have a valid Mainland visa or residence permit. You will need to pass a theory test, which we help you prepare for.

How long is the licence valid?

The first Mainland licence is valid for 6 years and can then be renewed.

Can I use it to drive in Hong Kong?

No. It is for driving in Mainland China only.
